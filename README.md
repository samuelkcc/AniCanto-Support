Public technical support for **AniCanto on iPhone and iPad**. The app is now available on the App Store.

[Download AniCanto on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/anicanto/id6828568012)

Dub Japanese anime in Cantonese, English or Mandarin, and learn one line at a time.
Supported models process video and audio on your device after setup. Check device requirements before downloading.

## Get help

Use the [mobile technical issue form](https://github.com/samuelkcc/AniCanto-Support/issues/new?template=technical-issue.yml).
You can write in English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, or 日本語.

Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, AniCanto version, and steps to reproduce the problem.
Reports are public. Do not post private videos, recordings, scripts, account details, receipts, or complete logs.

- [iPhone and iPad support guide](https://anicanto.com/support.html)
- [iPhone and iPad App privacy policy](https://anicanto.com/privacy.html)
- [Device requirements](https://anicanto.com/#requirements)
- [AniCanto website](https://anicanto.com/)

**macOS and Windows:** Use the [desktop project's own support page](https://github.com/samuelkcc/AniCanto/blob/main/SUPPORT.md) and [desktop issues](https://github.com/samuelkcc/AniCanto/issues).

This repository contains public support information and forms. It does not contain application or website source.
